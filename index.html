Privacy Policy – Cozy Life Planner

Last updated: April 7, 2026

1. Overview

Cozy Life Planner processes your personal data with respect for your privacy. All data is stored locally on your device and is never sent to any server.

2. Data Collected

The app stores the following data only on your device:

  • Profile information (username, level, XP)
  • Tasks, habits, and journal entries
  • Mood records and focus sessions
  • Photos added to journal entries
  • Water and sleep tracking data

3. Data Storage

All data is stored encrypted on your device using AES-256 encryption via the Hive database. Your data is never sent to any server.

4. Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat: Used for managing Cozy Plus subscriptions. Purchase history is processed by RevenueCat. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • App Store / Play Store: Platform API used for in-app reviews.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications: Task reminders and daily routines
  • Calendar: Optional device calendar sync
  • Camera / Photos: Adding photos to journal entries

6. Data Deletion

All data is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also manually delete all data from the Settings page.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13.
